Key points

Managing blood sugar is the key to living well with diabetes, and healthy eating is the key to managing blood sugar.
Simply put, eat healthy foods in the right amounts at the right times so your blood sugar stays in your target range as much as possible.
Work with your dietitian or diabetes educator to create a healthy eating plan and check out these resources to learn more.
